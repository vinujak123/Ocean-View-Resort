Getting Started

Welcome to the Ocean View Resort Management System. This portal is designed for efficient handling of room reservations and guest records.

📝

New Bookings

Navigate to "New Booking" to register a guest. Fill in their name, contact details, and stay dates. The system automatically calculates the bill based on room and board types.

📅

Reservations

View all current and past bookings in the "Reservations" tab. You can see guest details, stay periods, and total costs at a glance.

💳

Payments & Invoices

To generate a bill, go to "Payments", search by Reference ID, and click "Generate Invoice". You can print a hard copy directly from the browser.

📊

Analytics (Admins Only)

Admins can access "Financials" for detailed revenue reports, occupancy rates, and guest spending analysis.

Need technical assistance? Contact IT Support at support@oceanview.com
